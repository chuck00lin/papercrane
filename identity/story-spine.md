# Story spine

## 1. The reader
Who reads this, what they already believe, what they need to be able to do afterwards.

## 2. The gap
One paragraph. What is missing, stated so a reviewer in the field nods.

## 3. The positioning
How this work relates to the group's prior papers and to the two or three closest external works.

## 4. The promise
What the reader can do after reading that they could not before.

## 5. The narrative arc
Five nodes at most. Each node names the result section that carries it.

## 6. Research questions
Numbered. Each maps to at least one claim id.

## 7. Discussion arc
The order of discussion topics and the one sentence each must land.

## 8. Anti-pivot guardrails
What this paper will not become, written down so no late finding can quietly turn it into that.
